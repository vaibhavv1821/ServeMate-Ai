# 10 - Service Verification & Fraud Prevention

**STATUS: PLANNED** (Future Phase Feature)

---

## 1. Planned Security Safeguards
1. **OTP Verification**: Customer receives a 4-digit start OTP. Provider must enter the OTP into the application to transition booking from `ACCEPTED` to `IN_PROGRESS`.
2. **Before & After Service Proof**: Provider takes visual photos before starting and upon completing service. Photos are uploaded directly to Cloudinary and linked to the booking audit record.
